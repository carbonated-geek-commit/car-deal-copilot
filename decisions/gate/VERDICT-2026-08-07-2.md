## Why this is ESCALATE and not PASS

Three independent triggers, any one of which is sufficient under the gate's own rules:

- **A red line is weakened.** Consumer red line 7 is weakened twice over: the spec itself concedes the trail now carries weaker evidence (finding 8), and on the concierge rail the deliverable's proof degrades into operator self-attestation while the spec still claims proof is "captured automatically" (finding 9). Consumer red line 4's threading clause is weakened for the call channel (finding 7) — the SMS/email deferral is genuine sequencing and is NOT counted against it.
- **The spine is defined in more than one place.** 00 still mandates record/transcribe as shared behavior that 01 forbids, and 01 extends the shared Message and extraction contracts to cover an artifact 00 does not model (findings 12, 13).
- **Fidelity cannot be determined from the documents.** Whether transcription survives on B2B is unanswerable (finding 14), as is entitlement inheritance on a Q11 successor deal (finding 2) and the tenancy of the shared Dealership entity (finding 16).

Q13 is clean and introduces no unapproved integration or spend. Q11/Q12 are sound and strengthen the receipt trail. The escalation is driven by Q14 and by the fact that the v0.3 amendment landed in 01 without being carried into 00 or 02.
