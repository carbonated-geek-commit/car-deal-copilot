## Why this is PASS

None of the three escalation triggers fires.

**No red line is violated, and the one weakening is a legitimate PASS basis.** The verdict -3 trigger on consumer red line 1 is genuinely fixed, not papered over: Q20 gave the honesty promise a real referent by moving the fair-price verdict onto the one entity that can actually be priced, bound the valuation to it in the schema, made cross-thread ranking value-adjusted, and specified the honest failure mode. That is a stronger position than v0.3 held, not merely a restored one.

Consumer red line 7 at the concierge tier is the judgement call the assignment put to me, and I find the red line's substance survives. What the red line names — append-only, timestamped, exportable, and the concierge deliverable — is intact without exception. What is reduced is the evidentiary force of one channel: call-derived content on the losing threads. Four things carry that: the residue is narrower than the spec's own wording (finding 9 — dealer SMS and email in those threads remain primary artifacts); `Message.author` makes every operator-written entry visibly operator-written, so nothing is passed off as the dealer's; the concierge holds no signing authority and the winning deal is checkable against the dealership's own contract the customer signs; and the underlying trade is Q14, which this gate already ruled the principal's to make and which applies identically to a buyer typing their own notes on the $5 rail. Corban has now identified the exposure precisely, recorded it in the governing spec rather than the log, named its mitigations, and opened backlog item 7 for a verification mechanism. Under this gate's stated standard, an explicitly accepted, mitigated, and backlogged risk whose red line's substance survives is a PASS basis, and I so find. The sentence "This is a trust relationship, not an evidence chain" remains the sharpest edge in the specs; it is confined to the concierge tier by its context, and if it ever generalises beyond that tier the gate will treat it as a fresh weakening rather than as settled.

**The spine is defined once.** 00 owns the domain model, the valuation binding, the flag set, and the extraction contract; 01 and 02 restate but do not redefine. Two agreeing restatements are on watch (finding 20c) and neither contradicts its source.

**The consumer/B2B boundary holds.** v0.5 adds no divergence, the transcription question stays closed platform-wide, and no dealer-side revenue stream, feed, or ranking influence appears anywhere.

**Fidelity is determinable.** Every load-bearing question the last two verdicts could not answer from the documents now has an answer in the documents. What remains is calibration and editorial: an undeclared key, an unnamed valuation band, a stale table cell, and seat-level scoping — none of which any red line rests on.

Not counted against the specs: the Q19 billing deferral, the Q14 evidentiary trade, the absence of ADR-003..006 on main, and the task DAG's lag behind v0.5.
