## Why this is ESCALATE and not PASS

Two triggers, either sufficient under the gate's own rules:

- **A red line is weakened.** Consumer red line 1's substance — that the customer, who pays, gets an honest answer to "is this a good price?" — is undercut by the restructure: the deal's rationale claims a single known comparison vehicle, while the model it justifies fixes only make and model and lets year, trim, mileage, condition, and additions vary across threads under one deal-level walk-away number (findings 2, 3). Consumer red line 7's trust-engine clause is weakened at the concierge tier by an explicit statement that trust rides the relationship rather than the artifact (finding 6), with the residue concentrated in the unverifiable losing threads (finding 7).
- **Fidelity cannot be determined from the documents.** Nothing in 00 or 01 says what a `ValuationSnapshot` attaches to, or at what granularity walk-away and the flags are evaluated once `Deal.resolved_vehicle` is gone (finding 4) — a silence directly beneath the load-bearing claim the anchor design is built on.

Not counted against the specs: the Q19 billing deferral (declared and scoped, findings 10–11), the Q14 evidentiary trade (the principal's call, twice affirmed, finding 27), and the B2B side, which is clean throughout.

The v0.3 carry-through failures are genuinely fixed — findings 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, and 27 of the prior verdict are all closed, and the `author` field is a net gain to the thesis. The escalation is driven by the new model restructure, not by anything left over.
